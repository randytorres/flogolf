# Flogolf Owner Pitch - Low-Friction Version

Prepared March 21, 2026.

## Text To Send First

I took a look at Flogolf and I do not think the opportunity is a full software migration right away. The better move is building a lightweight internal ops layer on top of what you already use so bookings, lessons, memberships, events, and follow-up are not living in separate places. We can start small, keep downtime at basically zero, and break it into phases so you know the full cost without committing to everything on day one.

## If He Says "What Do You Mean?"

What I would build is not a rip-and-replace. It would start as a small internal system that gives you better visibility and automates the biggest leaks first, while your current tools keep running. Then if it proves useful, we expand it into the main operating layer for customer data, events, memberships, reporting, and eventually multi-location management.

## Short Proposal To Send

Flogolf already has the hard part done: demand, a strong experience, and multiple revenue lines. The gap is not the business. The gap is that bookings, memberships, lessons, events, leagues, and follow-up likely live across too many disconnected tools and manual steps.

My pitch is simple: do not try to replace everything at once. Start by adding a thin internal system on top of the current operation so you get better visibility, tighter follow-up, fewer dropped customers, and cleaner reporting without downtime or a risky migration.

That lets us improve the business in phases:

1. Start with the highest-leverage quick wins.
2. Prove value while current tools stay in place.
3. Expand only after the first phase is working.

## Simple Tiered Offer

### Tier 1 - Quick Wins + Owner Visibility

Best for getting started without a big commitment.

- Timeline: 2-3 weeks
- Price: $2,500-$4,000
- Goal: show immediate value without changing the whole business
- Includes: current-stack review, workflow map, one owner dashboard, and one high-impact workflow automation
- Best first workflow options: booking reminders/no-show prevention, membership activation follow-up, lesson intake follow-up, or event lead follow-up
- Outcome: you can start managing one important part of the business from a cleaner system right away
- Risk level: low

### Tier 2 - Core Ops Hub

Best for getting the main customer and revenue workflows under one roof.

- Timeline: 4-6 weeks
- Price: $6,000-$9,500
- Goal: give Flogolf a real internal operating layer
- Includes: customer 360, pipeline for leads and events, membership and lesson workflow automation, owner reporting, and bay-level visibility
- Outcome: less manual coordination, faster follow-up, cleaner customer history, and better revenue visibility
- Risk level: moderate but controlled because the current tools stay active during rollout

### Tier 3 - Revenue + Scale Layer

Best for turning the system into a long-term advantage.

- Timeline: 4-6 weeks
- Price: $5,000-$8,500
- Goal: make the system drive retention, repeat revenue, and expansion
- Includes: league workflows, win-back campaigns, referral/review flows, food and drink or upsell opportunities, and multi-location structure
- Outcome: stronger retention, more repeat revenue, and a cleaner path to a second location
- Risk level: low once the core hub is live

## Full Roadmap Pricing

- It does not ask him to shut anything down.
- It makes the first yes small.
- It gives him the full price picture up front.
- It sounds operational and realistic instead of "custom software for everything."
- It positions the work as revenue protection and operational control, not just automation.

- Existing payment/POS flows stay live at first.
- Staff can keep operating the current way while the new layer is added in parallel.
- Any migration happens only after a phase proves useful.

## Clean Reply If He Asks "Why Not Just Keep What We Have?"

Because the issue usually is not whether each tool works by itself. The issue is what gets lost between them. That is where lead follow-up, membership conversion, lesson retention, and event revenue usually leak. A lightweight internal system fixes the handoff problem without forcing a risky full migration.

## Clean Reply If He Asks "What Would You Start With?"

I would start with the smallest phase that gives you visible value fast: one owner view of the business plus one workflow that is probably leaking money today. That keeps the cost reasonable, proves the concept, and gives us a clean base to build on if you want to keep going.
