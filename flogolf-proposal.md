**Prepared for:** FlōGolf Lounge (Saugus)

## The Problem

FlōGolf already has the business working — bays, memberships, lessons, leagues, events, food and drink. The issue isn't demand. It's that each of those revenue lines runs through its own tool, its own workflow, and often its own manual follow-up.

That means:
- Customers slip between the cracks after buying a lesson or membership
- Event leads go cold without consistent follow-up
- Staff manually bridge gaps that should be automated
- There's no single view of customer history, revenue, or performance
- Scaling to a second location would multiply the mess

**This proposal fixes that — in phases, with zero disruption to your current operation.**

## How This Works

I'm not pitching a rip-and-replace. Nothing goes offline. Nothing breaks.

Each phase layers on top of what you already have. You keep every tool running until the new system proves it can handle that workflow better. Then we migrate, one piece at a time.

You approve each phase before it starts. You only pay for what you need, when you need it.
